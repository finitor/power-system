# 2026-08-01

## Power-balance model audit and operational rewrite

A critical audit found that the model's geometry correction and coefficient
bracket were sound improvements, but several output labels and operational
claims exceeded what the simulation actually computed.

Changes made:

- pinned the Open-Meteo archive request to ERA5 instead of the default
  best-match series, which can mix weather products across years;
- moved battery simulation from daily to hourly intervals, preserving midday
  saturation and overnight draw;
- replaced instantaneous empty-to-full "generator events" with the actual
  manual policy: 3.2 kW, planning start at configurable 20% SOC, stop at 90%;
- report generator kWh and hours as well as starts, while stating that 3.2 kW
  is presently a DC-bus-equivalent assumption with unmeasured conversion loss;
- separated median Oct-Apr net energy from gross negative-day energy. The
  latter measures storage-shifting pressure and is not a seasonal deficit;
- added explicit lean-caretaker stress cases for 2× heater duty and a 25%
  winter PV haircut;
- made usable battery capacity configurable and made Array 1 runs visibly
  provisional at both PR bracket endpoints;
- added focused unit tests for winter boundaries, hourly saturation,
  generator start/stop behavior, and winter derating.

The extended Array 0 calibration through August 1 used 469 bins and moved to
1.70 W per W/m² POA at 25 °C with R² 0.44. This remains inside the retained
1.60–2.01 bracket and reinforces that no running summer slope is a settled
point estimate.

The pinned-ERA5, hourly Array-0-only baseline gives lean unattended minimum
SOC of 75–77% across the coefficient bracket. At the low coefficient, 2×
heater duty reaches 35%, a 25% winter PV haircut reaches 69%, and both together
reach 16%; none empties the bank in the nine modeled winters. These are
conditional planning margins, not empirical winter guarantees.

Heater and on-site irradiance instrumentation require hardware changes and are
deferred until summer 2027 or later. The first defensible full-system empirical
run is gated on Array 1 commissioning in summer 2027; until then `--array1` is
explicitly a design-sensitivity mode.
