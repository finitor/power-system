## Winter power-balance model

> Canonical, maintained version of this analysis:
> [docs/power-budget.md](../power-budget.md). This entry records the day's
> work; the power-budget doc carries the current numbers and open
> measurements.

Built an annual power-balance model from the first ~13 days of full telemetry
(2026-06-20 onward) plus 10 years of Open-Meteo/ERA5 irradiance for the site
grid cell (47.952, -84.841): daily GHI 2016-2025, and hourly 45°-south
plane-of-array irradiance for the mounted-array scenarios.

Calibration notes:

- Summer production is curtailment-bound (bank hits 100% SOC almost daily),
  so daily energy counters cannot calibrate PV *potential*. Calibrated on
  10-minute bins restricted to Bulk stage + SOC ≤ 88% (BMS charge limit
  ~10.6 kW never binding): **1.84 W delivered DC per W/m² of GHI** system
  wide — array 0 1.27 (~53% of nameplate per unit GHI), array 1 flat on the
  ground only 0.57 (~16%; flat + tight tree horizon + vegetation
  encroachment).
- Measured load 5.15 kWh/day (214 W). Overnight floor ~95 W decomposes as
  Starlink gen 2 (~34 W DC-side) + Magnum no-load (~44 W) + Pi/comms
  (~15 W). Refrigeration ≈ 2.6 kWh/day of the total.
- `epever.1 generated_today` is a cumulative counter, not daily. Query the
  live metrics DB with `mode=ro`, not `immutable=1` (spurious
  "database disk image is malformed" mid-scan while the supervisor writes).

Headline results (scripts: [`calibrate_pv.py`](../../scripts/calibrate_pv.py)
runs on the Pi against the metrics DB, [`annual_model.py`](../../scripts/annual_model.py)
against downloaded irradiance; heater modeled at 200 W with
temperature-scaled duty):

- As configured in June (array 1 flat): structural deficit mid-Oct through
  Feb, ~410 kWh/winter at full load. Not viable without a generator.
- Array 1 at 45° south changes late-Dec resource from ~0.87 to
  ~1.58 kWh/m²/day — but two-thirds of that is direct beam, which the site
  partially loses (below).
- **Lean unattended caretaker mode is robust in every scenario examined**:
  Pi (15 W) + one 60-minute daily inverter+Starlink telemetry-upload window
  (~0.1 kWh/day; supervisor toggles the Magnum — the one Magnum write that
  works) + heater. Bank never went below ~92% SOC across 9 simulated winters
  with the array mounted; ~64% even with array 1 contributing nothing. The
  60-minute window is a rounding error; the battery heater dominates the
  winter budget and its real duty cycle is the number to measure this
  winter. 2x the assumed heater duty is the failure mode (cold-locked bank).
- Occupied no-fridge winter (~2.3 kWh/day) rides through on the bank even
  with December beam mostly occluded: worst case across 9 winters was one
  0.8 kWh shortfall event. Strictly net-positive darkest weeks would need
  the roofline sightline cleared; functionally it barely matters.

## Array 1 rewired 3s4p (dry run)

Decision: **3s4p, one 3s string per physical row**, replacing the planned
4s3p — full rationale and electrical envelope now in
[the charge-controller doc](../subsystems/charge-controller.md#array-1-string-topology-3s4p-decided-2026-07-02).
Short version: the roofline shadow edge is horizontal and sweeps bottom-up;
row-pure parallel strings degrade proportionally (N shaded rows = N/4 output
lost) where 4s columns would deliver zero with three rows shaded. All four
strings on one EPEver input (33 A of the 50 A limit), four 15 A string fuses,
PV2 reserved for the provisional third array. Known accepted trade-offs: thin
code margin on the buried 70 ft 8 AWG feeder (design ~55 A vs 50-55 A rating;
physically self-limiting at ~35 A) and ~2.7% peak-hour voltage drop.

Dry-run wired on the ground today. Things to watch in telemetry over the
coming weeks:

- `epever.1 pv_voltage` should now read ~108 V Vmp-class instead of ~144 V.
- Re-run the uncurtailed-bin calibration after a week: does the per-kW
  effectiveness move off the flat-layout 16% floor once wiring/self-shading
  changes, before the platform even goes up?
- After platform mount: a clear-day `epever.1 pv_power` trace gives the
  roofline occlusion profile directly (midday notch vs clean bell), replacing
  the AR estimate with measurement.

## Battery expansion question (200 Ah vs 400 Ah)

Evaluated whether doubling the bank changes any off-season outcome:
**no — winter is energy-limited, not storage-limited.** Doubling halves
generator *starts* at full occupied load (same energy), and buys real depth in
exactly one corner (array-failure + underestimated-heater double fault in
unattended mode). Decision: collect a full season of data first. Mixed-age
parallel LFP packs are not a blocker for waiting (the "never mix ages" rule is
a lead-acid series-string inheritance). Full analysis and expansion caveats:
[power-budget doc](../power-budget.md#battery-capacity-200-ah-vs-400-ah-evaluated-2026-07-02).
