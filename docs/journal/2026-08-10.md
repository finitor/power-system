# 2026-08-10 — Production battery-heater gate

Restored the reactive heater temperature band from the 2026-06-26 bench values
(17.5/20 °C) to the production 2/5 °C cut-in/cut-out band.

The original 132/130 V Classic VOC gate was not restored. That gate had been
derived while array 0 was unknowingly wired as mismatched 4s∥3s strings. A
consistent snapshot of post-correction telemetry (2026-07-19 through
2026-08-10, Bulk samples) showed the useful raw summer VOC range had moved to
roughly 164–172 V:

| Raw `last_voc` threshold | Samples ≥ 200 W | Samples ≥ 400 W |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 164 V | 91.3% | 66.9% |
| 166 V | 97.6% | 76.1% |
| 168 V | 99.5% | 86.6% |
| 170 V | 99.8% | 95.8% |

A fixed voltage is not suitable for winter because module VOC rises about
0.34% per degree C below 25 °C. The first implementation normalized against
Open-Meteo outdoor temperature at 164/160 V, but live verification rejected it:
166.8 V VOC normalized to only 162.4 V while the array was actually producing
about 1.6 kW, and requiring fresh internet weather conflicted with the
store-and-forward operating case.

The corrected production loop uses the existing local ambient probe and no
external service:

```text
normalized_VOC = measured_VOC / (1 + 0.0034 × (25 − local_ambient_temperature_C))
```

Reactive heat qualifies at normalized VOC ≥ 158 V continuously for 60 seconds
and releases below 154 V. Missing battery, Classic, or local ambient telemetry
fails reactive heat off. Two live conditions verified the final threshold:
166.8 V / 19.3 °C normalized to 163.6 V at about 1.6 kW, and 161.8 V /
19.4 °C normalized to 158.8 V at 862 W.

Re-querying the post-correction data with the local ambient series showed
normalized VOC ≥ 158 V coinciding with at least 200 W in 79.1% and at least
400 W in 56.4% of Bulk samples, with 639 W average output. The 60-second
qualification and 4 V release hysteresis trade brief false positives for
earlier cold-lock recovery.
