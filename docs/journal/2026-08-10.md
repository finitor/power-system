# 2026-08-10 — Production battery-heater gate

Restored the reactive heater temperature band from the 2026-06-26 bench values
(17.5/20 °C) to the production 2/5 °C cut-in/cut-out band.

The original 132/130 V Classic VOC gate was not restored. That gate had been
derived while array 0 was unknowingly wired as mismatched 4s∥3s strings. A
consistent snapshot of post-correction telemetry (2026-07-19 through
2026-08-10, Bulk samples) showed the useful raw summer VOC range had moved to
roughly 164–172 V:

| Raw `last_voc` threshold | Samples ≥ 200 W | Samples ≥ 400 W |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 164 V | 91.3% | 66.9% |
| 166 V | 97.6% | 76.1% |
| 168 V | 99.5% | 86.6% |
| 170 V | 99.8% | 95.8% |

A fixed voltage is not suitable for winter because module VOC rises about
0.34% per degree C below 25 °C. The production loop now normalizes Classic
`last_voc` to a 25 °C outdoor reference:

```text
normalized_VOC = measured_VOC / (1 + 0.0034 × (25 − outdoor_temperature_C))
```

Reactive heat qualifies at normalized VOC ≥ 164 V continuously for 60 seconds
and releases below 160 V. The outdoor temperature comes from the already-cached
Open-Meteo report; it must be non-stale and no more than one hour old. Missing
battery, Classic, or usable weather telemetry fails reactive heat off. The
equivalent measured thresholds are approximately 168/164 V at 18 °C,
178/174 V at 0 °C, and 189/184 V at −20 °C.

With outdoor-temperature normalization, the same post-correction data showed
normalized VOC ≥ 164 V coinciding with at least 200 W in 99.7% and at least
400 W in 95.3% of Bulk samples.
