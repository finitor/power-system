# 2026-06-17 — Charge allocator: scaffold to live, and the fallout it surfaced

The through-line was taking the charge-current allocator from the operator's
early scaffold (`b077a2e`) to **live current-limit authority**, and fixing the
several things that surfaced along the way. Design reference is
[charge-current-allocation.md](../charge-current-allocation.md), rewritten today
to the as-implemented spec + operator controls.

## The allocator, scaffold → live

Reviewed the scaffold and folded in the design points, then built it out:

- **Weighting** (`236f9ea`): apportion by **PV input power**, not throttled
  output — weighting by present output is a latch (throttle → low output → low
  weight → stays throttled), and it bites exactly when the budget binds. Plus
  sub-floor `disable` (EPEver 1 A register floor → coil off) and an eligibility
  contract: "able to charge" means PV present, **not** currently sourcing.
- **Dry-run telemetry** (`e229f48`, `d123fe7`, `95f08ab`): each cycle logs a
  `charge_allocator`/`allocation_decision` event, throttled to material change +
  heartbeat so it doesn't flood the event store.
- **Phase 1 — ceiling port** (`0f6ca47`): `charge_ceiling.py` ports the legacy
  taper's top-knee voltage/SOC ramp **and** the safety behaviors (high-cell-V
  stop, cell-delta stop, full-charge latch) as a ceiling on *total* net charge
  current, combined with the BMS CCL via `min()`. Not a per-controller cap (that
  would double near full). The stateful latch lives in the ceiling object; the
  allocator stays pure.
- **Phase 3 — live writes** (`f33d755`, `3d281f8`): `--charge-allocation` makes
  the allocator the **sole** current-limit writer (Classic volatile limit,
  EPEver register + charge coil). Mutual exclusion with the live taper enforced
  at startup. No limit-write rate-limiting (a limit is a ceiling; controllers
  ramp via their own CV/soft-start).
- **Unconstrained short-circuit** (`bdc1088`): if `Σ(charger max) ≤ CCL` the
  allocator isn't the binding constraint (sunlight is) — pin each to its max, no
  reserve, no apportionment, no writes. This is the normal daytime state and
  stops needless EEPROM writes; it answered "why is the EPEver limit 99 A this
  early?" (199 budget split two ways).
- **Operator surface** (`3de8e40`): `CHARGE_ALLOC_*` / `CHARGE_CEILING_*` env
  overrides for tuning; the on/off **toggle stays the version-controlled unit
  flag, deliberately not env** (see the taper lesson below). A "Charge
  Allocation" panel was added to the terminal display.

## The legacy taper was running live via a stray env var

Chasing "the EPEver current limit keeps resetting to 10 A on its own," found the
old `charger_taper` running **live, target=epever**, writing `0x9013` — enabled
by `CHARGER_CURRENT_TAPER=true` in `/etc/offgrid-power.env`, **not** in any
committed config. `deploy.sh` re-rendering the unit is what had been silently
toggling it. Disabled the env, made the allocator the sole writer.

Lesson, now baked into the design: **control-authority toggles stay
version-controlled** (the `--charge-allocation` flag in the unit), so "is it
secretly acting on the hardware?" is always answerable from git. Tuning *values*
can live in env; on/off authority cannot.

## EPEver daily energy: derive from the lifetime counter

The EPEver "generated today" register **does not reset at local midnight** (the
RTC we set didn't stick — dead backup). The store proved it: flat across 00:00,
resumed climbing at sunrise. Its **lifetime total is monotonic and reliable**,
so "Production Today" is now `generated_total(now) − generated_total(midnight)`,
differenced over a boundary we control (`MetricRecorder.midnight_metric_value`,
`ce48117`). Same trick the Load cumulative now uses.

## Load group overhaul

- **Cumulative Today** (`97ad074`): was single-source (Classic Ah only) and went
  negative when the EPEver produced. Replaced with an **energy balance of both
  producers**: `Classic_kWh + EPEver_kWh − battery_gain` (battery gain = SOCΔ ×
  capacity × nominal V, the only place voltage enters). Returns unavailable
  rather than undercount if a producer's daily is missing.
- **Autonomy** left as-is: it already rides the corrected rolling-average load,
  and per operator call it counts to empty (a discharge floor is theoretical
  given daytime charging).
- **Display** (`bde2f98`): adaptive Wh/kWh — small figures read in Wh, ≥1 kWh in
  kWh — applied to both Production Today and Cumulative Today. Honest that the
  counters quantize to 10 Wh (EPEver) / 100 Wh (Classic), so it's a unit change
  for readability, not added precision.

## Magnum read failures: not the EPEver

Read failures rose sharply since the EPEver PV was connected. Tested the "EPEver
switching noise" hypothesis against a day of data (journal failure timestamps vs
stored EPEver output, hourly): **refuted** — failures are present around the
clock and *highest overnight when the EPEver is fully idle* (0 W → 50–67/hr) and
not elevated at peak charging (217 W → 44/hr); 85% occur at ≤1 A. The onset is
tied to the *physical* PV attachment, so the cause is likely wiring/grounding/
adapter, not charging current. Raised `max_cycles` 10→20 to quiet the warnings
(`f005c2c`); physical troubleshooting tracked as engineering-plan **item 12**.

## Phase 2: first ceiling re-tune

Replicated the ceiling formula against a real charge cycle: the bank charges
**53–56.5 V (median 55.1)**, so the inherited voltage thresholds (53.6/54.4/54.8)
sat *below* the operating point and **over-clamped 18% of charging samples to
~4 A while the bank took up to ~13 A at 88–95% SOC**. The SOC ramp was fine —
only the voltage ramp was miscalibrated. Re-tuned via env (`a2441b2`):
`BULK_VOLTAGE 55.0 / RAMP2 55.8 / TOP 56.4 / FULL_RESET 54.5`. To validate over a
full charge to the 55–56 V plateau, then promote to `ChargeCeilingConfig`
defaults.
