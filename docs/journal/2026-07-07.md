# 2026-07-07

## USB RS485 rebinding after hub move

Moved the two serial-less CH340 RS485 adapters onto the powered USB hub and
re-keyed their udev identities by the new physical paths:

| Device | Symlink | Path | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Magnum network tap | `/dev/magnum-rs485` | `platform-3f980000.usb-usb-0:1.3.2:1.0` | Magnum-only supervisor read decoded inverter status |
| EPEver COM adapter | `/dev/epever-rs485` | `platform-3f980000.usb-usb-0:1.3.3:1.0` | `scripts/epever-probe.py --device /dev/ttyUSB1` read Modbus registers at 115200 baud |

Before rebinding, the installed rules still expected Magnum on `1.2` and
EPEver on `1.5.3`, so neither canonical symlink existed after the move and the
supervisor logged repeated `/dev/epever-rs485` missing errors. Reloaded udev,
triggered tty devices, and restarted `offgrid-supervisor`; the API returned
status OK with Classic, EPEver, Magnum, and battery telemetry present.

The CH340 adapters still share VID:PID and generic serial
`1a86_USB2.0-Serial`, so identity continues to follow hub port, not adapter
body. Keep physical labels on the hub ports and adapter leads.
