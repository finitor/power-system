## EPEver TEP10425 on hand

Manual mined: native LFP16S profile is hotter than our practice;
user-define 36–62 V covers conservative setpoints; COM port pinout
(6=A, 3=B, 8=GND, 1=+5V keep clear); Pylon batteries connect *directly*
to port 9 as BMS protocol 21 — no BMS-Link module. The native closed-loop
BMS mode (BPRO/UBS) is NOT usable as policy here on present evidence:
1,487 snapshots show the Cubix publishes a static 58.4 V/200 A protection
envelope, never modulating. Supervisor stays the single charge-policy
authority; deciding bench observation is frame 0x351 through a real
full-charge.

Consolidating both arrays on the EPEver's dual input: considered, parked.
Redundancy + nine years of Classic reliability win; the dual input is the
Classic-failure contingency. Read-only `scripts/epever-probe.py` staged
for the bench (borrow the SH-U11H; Magnum path is read-only anyway).

## CAN outage with a lesson

Battery CAN went fully silent; adapter reset didn't help; Bluetooth app
coincidentally unreachable too, which fed a wrong BMS-comm-crash theory.
Actual cause: loose CAN-H wire at the adapter screw terminal. The
diagnostic hole, now in troubleshooting.md: a listen-only adapter on an
open wire shows zero frames AND zero errors — indistinguishable from a
quiet bus. Check terminals first. Degraded mode worked as designed
throughout (last-good displays, honest staleness, no restarts needed).
