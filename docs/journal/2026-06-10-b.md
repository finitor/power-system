# 2026-06-10-b — Toggle test, LBCO, Kindle durability, taper in motion

Second session of the day, after the public release.

## Magnum inverter toggle: remote does not interfere

Live test with AC loads briefly dropped: one Pi-sent toggle turned the
inverter OFF; it stayed off for 12 s with the ME-RC50 connected and silent
(ctrl byte 0x00 throughout); the Pi's restore toggle brought it back. The
on/off toggle is a transition command, not rebroadcast state — the
opposite of charge settings. Banked as a known capability (low-voltage
inverter disable is achievable with the remote installed), deliberately
not built. Recorded in the research note and as an addendum on decision
0002, whose rejection stands and gained its final rationale: generator
charging is always human-attended, so closed-loop control effort belongs
on the unattended solar path.

## Bottom-end protection settled

ME-RC50 LBCO read at the panel: 48.0 V — exactly the recommended 3.0 V/cell,
~3.2 V above the BMS's 44.8 V disconnect. Cut-in is firmware-fixed (not a
menu item). Documented with rationale in the inverter-charger subsystem
doc. `InverterEventTracker` now logs inverter on/off transitions to a
durable CSV, classifying off-transitions with LOW_BAT/DEAD_BAT faults as
`lbco_cutout` with battery context — real cut-outs are captured whenever
they happen.

## Taper validation in motion

With overcast weather forecast all week, validation can't wait for live
threshold crossings: `scripts/taper-sim.py` sweeps a synthetic charge day
through the real controller (review passed; ramp-boundary steps deemed
fine), and every actionable decision logs to a durable CSV. The dry-run
caught a real bug on its first decision: bulk targeted 100 A over the
operator's 80 A panel limit. `bulk_current_a` is now the operator ceiling;
the taper only ever reduces from it.

## Kindle wall display: durable at last

The reboot-stranding problem (browser error page kills meta-refresh) went
through a failed detour — three iframe-loader variants on the mesquite
engine: two blank renders (zero-height body quirks), one mis-sized — before
landing on the right architecture: the served pages replace `<meta refresh>`
with an in-place XHR refresher that fetches and swaps body content without
ever navigating. One self-referential bug en route: the script's own regex
literal matched itself in the fetched head and spliced script source into
the display; fixed with split-string markers. Verified end to end: Pi
reboot with hands off the Kindle, display froze stale and self-recovered.
A side benefit: outages now show last-good data with a frozen Updated
stamp instead of a blank screen.

Terminal displays switched from "Refreshed: NN seconds ago" to the same
"Updated: HH:MM:SS TZ" stamp as the web pages.

## Eco-Worthy RS485 opens up

A generic FT232R USB-RS485 cable + Eco-Worthy's Windows Companion software
connected to the Cubix pack: per-cell voltages and temperatures visible in
its frame monitor (richer than the CAN broadcast), maintenance functions
behind a write lock. RS485 stays unnecessary as a fallback but is now the
proven path to cell-level granularity if a need appears. New research note:
ecoworthy-rs485-interface.md.
